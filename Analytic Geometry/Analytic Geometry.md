# Analytic Geometry

## Summary

Analytic geometry studies geometric objects with coordinates, equations, and vectors. Distance, midpoints, lines, planes, vectors, and conic sections connect algebra to geometry and prepare the way for multivariable calculus and linear algebra.

## Prerequisites

[[Functions]] and basic coordinate geometry. Helpful: [[Trigonometry]], [[Completing the Square]].

## Learning order

1. [[Distance and Midpoint]] — metric structure of the plane and space
2. [[Completing the Square]] — rewrite quadratics for centers and radii
3. [[Lines and Planes]] — parametric and Cartesian forms
4. [[Vectors and Dot Product]] — direction, length, angles, projections
5. [[Conic Sections]] — circle, ellipse, parabola, hyperbola

## How to use these notes

- Prefer equations with stated coordinate systems.
- Check assumptions (dimension, nonzero normals/directions) before applying formulas.
- Use completing the square when identifying centers of circles and ellipses.

## Next steps

- Linear algebra: [[Linear Algebra]], especially [[Vector Spaces and Bases]] and [[Orthogonality and Projections]]
- Calculus: multivariable topics under [[Calculus]]
